Roformer vocal separation and cleanup models

attend.py, bs_roformer.py, mel_band_roformer.py and the vocal separation configs in configs/
(mel_roformer_kim.yaml, bs_roformer_viperx.yaml) are taken from
Music-Source-Separation-Training by Roman Solovyev (ZFTurbo):
  https://github.com/ZFTurbo/Music-Source-Separation-Training
whose Roformer models build on BS-RoFormer by Phil Wang (lucidrains):
  https://github.com/lucidrains/BS-RoFormer
Both are released under the MIT licence (texts below). Each file says what was changed.

The cleanup configs (dereverb_mbr_anvuew.yaml, dereverb_echo_mbr_fused.yaml) list the model
settings of the dereverb checkpoints by anvuew and by Sucial and the MSST-WebUI community, as
MSST-WebUI publishes them next to the checkpoints it hosts:
  https://github.com/SUC-DriverOld/MSST-WebUI
  https://huggingface.co/Sucial/MSST-WebUI
Only the settings needed to build the models are kept.

The model weights (checkpoints) are not part of this folder. They are downloaded or imported
separately; their authors have not declared a licence for them.
